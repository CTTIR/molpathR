# molpathR: Molecular Pathology Data Platform for Clinical NGS Integration

Ingests, links, queries and visualizes molecular pathology data from
heterogeneous sources, including next-generation sequencing (NGS) files
(FASTQ, BAM, VCF), XML variant interpretation reports, structured
pathology reports in PDF format, exports from pathology and clinical
information systems, and survival outcome data. Provides a searchable
in-memory database and an interactive 'shiny' application for clinical
exploration and cohort analysis. Raw sequence and alignment readers do
not perform variant calling or clinical interpretation. The optional
'Bioconductor' packages 'Rsamtools', 'ShortRead' and 'Biostrings' are
used when installed.
